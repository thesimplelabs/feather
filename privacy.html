Privacy policy

For Feather by Simple Labs · Version 1.0.0 · Last updated: October 10, 2026

Feather is a browser extension that adds tools to supported ChatGPT and Claude pages. This policy explains how the extension handles information.

Conversation content

Feather's features read conversation content on supported ChatGPT and Claude pages to provide tools such as search, prompt navigation, bookmarks, exports, and context estimates. Conversation content is processed in the browser by Feather. The extension does not send conversation content to a server operated by Simple Labs.

Information stored locally

Feather uses Chrome's extension storage for settings such as whether the extension is enabled, the active-message limit, theme preference, keyboard shortcuts, and bookmarks. Bookmarks may include identifiers associated with a conversation and message so Feather can return to saved responses. This information is stored in the browser's extension storage.

Exports

If you choose to export a conversation, Feather creates the requested file in your browser and uses the browser's download or print-to-PDF functionality. You control whether and where to save the exported file.

Analytics, advertising, and sale of data

Feather is not designed to include analytics or advertising trackers. Simple Labs does not sell conversation content or use it for advertising.

Third-party platforms

Feather operates on ChatGPT and Claude pages. Those services are operated by OpenAI and Anthropic respectively and are governed by their own privacy policies and practices. Feather does not control what those platforms collect or how they process data.

Permissions

Feather requests access to supported ChatGPT and Claude pages to provide its features, and browser storage to save settings and bookmarks. Browser permissions are managed by Chrome.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Feather's features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact

Need help with Feather or found a bug? Visit our GitHub Issues page to report a problem or request support.
