
# Feather by Simple Labs

**Make AI chats lighter.**

Feather is a free browser extension that makes conversations on ChatGPT and Claude easier to navigate, search, and manage.

🌐 **Website:** https://thesimplelabs.github.io/feather/

🛠️ **Support:** https://github.com/thesimplelabs/feather/issues

🔒 **Privacy Policy:** https://thesimplelabs.github.io/feather/privacy.html

---

## ✨ Features

- **Chat Search**: Find messages and information in your conversations.
- **Prompt Navigation**: Quickly find and navigate between prompts.
- **Bookmarks**: Save important responses for later.
- **Context Meter**: View an estimate of your conversation's context usage.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Navigate supported features more efficiently.
- **Adaptive Themes**: Match the appearance of supported chat platforms.

## 🆓 Free, by design

Feather is free to use. No subscription or account is required.

Optional donations help support continued development: [Support me on Ko-fi ☕](https://ko-fi.com/simplelabs).

## 🔐 Privacy first

Feather is designed to process conversation content locally in your browser rather than sending it to Simple Labs servers.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://thesimplelabs.github.io/feather/privacy.html) for more information.
